# Fictional Job Description — Medical AI Product Associate

## Responsibilities
- Translate clinical and user needs into product requirements and MVP definitions for healthcare AI products.
- Support evaluation of LLM or generative AI features, including hallucination and clinical-safety risks.
- Use Python or structured workflows to automate analysis and improve traceability of outputs.
- Communicate scientific evidence clearly to cross-functional product and clinical stakeholders.
- Coordinate product work across clinical, engineering, and design partners.

## Requirements
- Use data analysis to identify patterns and turn findings into actionable insights.
- Plan milestones, risks, scope, and trade-offs for prototype delivery.
- Create clear internal product training or enablement materials.

## Preferred
- Experience owning enterprise production deployments and commercial revenue targets.
